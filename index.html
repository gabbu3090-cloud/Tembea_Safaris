Welcome to Explore Kenya! Discover Kenya's incredible wildlife, snow-capped mountains, vast savannahs, and rich culture.

Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is highest in Kenya, second in Africa. Famous for snow peaks Batian, Nelion, Lenana. UNESCO site. Activities: hiking, climbing, camping, cultural tours.

Learn more

2. Amboseli National Park

Amboseli in southern Kenya near Tanzania. Best for large elephant herds with 1500 elephants. Iconic view of Kilimanjaro. See lions, cheetahs, giraffes, 400+ birds.

Learn more

3. Maasai Mara

Most famous park in southwest Kenya. Known for Great Migration of 1.5M wildebeest July-Oct. Has Big Five. Enjoy game drives, balloon safaris, Maasai culture.

Learn more

4. Tsavo National Park

One of largest parks in world, East and West. Famous for red elephants and maneless lions. Features Mzima Springs, Shetani Lava Flows. 500+ bird species.
