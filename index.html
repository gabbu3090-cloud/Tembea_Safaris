Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is the highest mountain in Kenya and the second-highest in Africa after Kilimanjaro. It is famous for its three snow-capped peaks - Batian, Nelion and Lenana - and beautiful forests. The mountain is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. It is home to unique wildlife like elephants, buffaloes and monkeys. Visitors can enjoy hiking, mountain climbing, camping, nature walks, and learning about the culture of the Kikuyu, Embu and Meru people who live around it.

Learn more about Mount Kenya

2. Amboseli National Park

Amboseli National Park is located in southern Kenya near the Tanzania border. It is famously known as the best place in Africa to get close to large herds of free-ranging elephants, with over 1,500 elephants. The park offers the most iconic view of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest mountain, as a perfect backdrop. Apart from elephants, you can see lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras and over 400 species of birds. Activities include game drives, bird watching, visiting Observation Hill, and cultural visits to Maasai villages.

Learn more about Amboseli National Park

3. Maasai Mara National Reserve

Maasai Mara National Reserve is Kenya's most famous and visited park, located in the southwest. It is best known for the Great Wildebeest Migration, where over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras and gazelles cross from Tanzania every year between July and October. The reserve has the Big Five - lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino. Visitors can enjoy thrilling game drives, hot air balloon safaris at sunrise, photography, and cultural visits to Maasai villages to learn about their traditional way of life.

Learn more about Maasai Mara

4. Tsavo National Park

Tsavo National Park is one of the largest national parks in the world, divided into Tsavo East and Tsavo West. It is located in southeastern Kenya. Tsavo is famous for its large populations of red elephants, which get their color from rolling in the red soil, as well as maneless lions and diverse landscapes. The park has beautiful features like Mzima Springs, Shetani Lava Flows, and Yatta Plateau. Visitors can enjoy long game drives, bird watching with over 500 bird species, photography, and seeing the amazing wilderness without crowds.
