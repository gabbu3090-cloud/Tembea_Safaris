Explore Kenya

Welcome to Explore Kenya! Discover the beauty of Kenya, a wonderful tourist destination in East Africa known for its incredible wildlife, snow-capped mountains, vast savannahs, and rich culture.

Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Mount Kenya

Mount Kenya is the highest mountain in Kenya and second-highest in Africa. Famous for its snow-capped peaks Batian, Nelion and Lenana. It is a UNESCO World Heritage Site. Visitors enjoy hiking, climbing, camping and cultural tours.

Learn more about Mount Kenya

2. Amboseli National Park

Amboseli is in southern Kenya near Tanzania. Best place to see large herds of elephants with over 1500 elephants. Famous for iconic views of Mount Kilimanjaro. You can see lions, cheetahs, giraffes and 400+ birds. Activities: game drives and Maasai village visits.

Learn more about Amboseli

3. Maasai Mara National Reserve

Maasai Mara is Kenya's most famous park in southwest Kenya. Known for Great Wildebeest Migration where 1.5 million wildebeest cross yearly July-Oct. Has Big Five - lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo, rhino. Enjoy game drives, hot air balloon safaris and Maasai culture.

Learn more about Maasai Mara

4. Tsavo National Park

Tsavo is one of largest parks in world, divided into East and West in southeast Kenya. Famous for red elephants that roll in red soil and maneless lions. Features Mzima Springs and Shetani Lava Flows. Enjoy game drives and bird watching with 500+ species.

Learn more about Tsavo
