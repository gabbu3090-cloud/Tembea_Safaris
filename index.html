Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Maasai Mara National Reserve

Maasai Mara National Reserve is Kenya's most famous and visited park, located in the southwest. It is best known for the Great Wildebeest Migration, where over 1.5 million wildebeest, zebras and gazelles cross from Tanzania every year between July and October. The reserve has the Big Five - lion, leopard, elephant, buffalo and rhino. Visitors can enjoy thrilling game drives, hot air balloon safaris at sunrise, and cultural visits to Maasai villages to learn about their traditional way of life.

Learn more about Maasai Mara

2. Diani Beach - Coastal Paradise

Diani Beach is located on the south coast of Kenya, 30km south of Mombasa. It has been voted Africa's best beach destination for many years. The beach is famous for its 17km of soft white sand, crystal-clear turquoise water, swaying palm trees and coral reefs. Tourists can enjoy swimming, snorkeling, scuba diving, dolphin watching, skydiving, and dhow sailing. The nearby Shimba Hills National Reserve and Wasini Island make it a perfect place for both relaxation and adventure.

Learn more about Diani Beach

3. Amboseli National Park - Home of Elephants

Amboseli National Park is located in southern Kenya near the Tanzania border. It is famously known as the best place in Africa to get close to large herds of free-ranging elephants, with over 1,500 elephants. The park offers the most iconic view of Mount Kilimanjaro, Africa's highest mountain, as a backdrop. Apart from elephants, you can see lions, cheetahs, giraffes, zebras and over 400 species of birds. Activities include game drives, bird watching, visiting observation hill for a full view of the park, and experiencing Maasai culture. It is a photographer's paradise.
